### ⏱️ Step 1: Phase Sync (5 min)
* **Symptoms:** "The vibe is off," "Irritation is contagious," "We are out of sync."
    * *Physics:* Phase Delta $\Delta\theta > 25^\circ$
* **Goal:** Correct phase misalignment and re-establish connection.

1.  **Stop:** Cease all conversation and movement.
2.  **Breathe (60s):** Match your breathing rhythm exactly to your partner's.
3.  **Silence (30s):** Sit side-by-side (do not face head-on). No eye contact required.
4.  **Sync:** Share your *state* only, not your *opinion*.
    * *Say:* "I just want to be near you right now." or "We need to settle down." (Do not debate).

### ⏱️ Step 2: R-Drop (15 min)
* **Symptoms:** Partner is defensive, aggressive, or withdrawn.
    * *Physics:* Resistance $R_B > 1.2$ (High Friction)
* **Goal:** Disarm the partner's resistance mechanism.

1.  **Listen:** **Rebuttals are prohibited.** Listen to their words purely as audio input.
2.  **Observe:** Acknowledge facts without evaluation.
    * *Say:* "So [X] happened, and you are saying [Y]."
3.  **Valid:** Validate the emotion.
    * *Say:* "I see why you felt that way," or "That must have been painful."
4.  **Wait:** Wait for the **Exhale** (the physiological sign that they feel understood).

    * Drink tea together.
    * **Objective:** Immerse fully in "The Two-Body System" (Your shared world).
